# 🟡 Blinkit Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing Blinkit grocery sales across items, outlets and customer ratings.


## 📌 KPIs
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | 1.20M |
| Average Rating | 3.9 |
| No. of Items | 9K |
| Average Sales | 141 |

## 🎛 Interactive Features
- **Metric switch buttons** (built with a Field Parameter) to change every visual between Total Sales, Average Sales, No. of Items and Average Rating
- **Slicers:** Outlet Location, Outlet Size, Item Type
- **Cross-filtering** across all visuals

## 📊 Visuals Used
- Donut chart: Fat content split
- Bar charts: Item type, Fat by outlet tier
- Area chart: Sales by outlet establishment year
- Stacked column chart: Outlet type and size
- Scatter plot: Item visibility vs. sales

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Supermarket Type1 contributes roughly 66% of total sales
- Low Fat items drive about 65% of total sales
- Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods are the top-selling item types
- Tier 3 outlets lead in sales across both fat categories
- Higher shelf visibility does not necessarily mean higher sales
